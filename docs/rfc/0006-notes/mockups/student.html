The ratio of squares to circles is 3∶4. There are 12 circles. How many squares?

  1. The ratio 3∶4 means 3 squares for every 4 circles.
  2. 12 circles = 4 × 3 groups, so scale the squares by 3 too.
  3. 3 squares × 3 groups = 9 squares.
